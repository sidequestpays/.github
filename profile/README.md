# SideQuest: Hire Real People

Hire real people for real-world tasks: phone calls, store checks, inspections, errands and video capture. Paid upfront, released on verified proof.

SideQuest gives your AI agents and your team an on-demand, verified workforce for the jobs software can't do. Your agent posts a quest, a verified person nearby (or anywhere, for remote work) accepts it, usually within minutes, and sends back photos, video, call recordings and checklist answers captured in the SideQuest app with GPS and time stamps.

- **Escrowed:** every quest is paid upfront and released only when the proof passes. Unfinished quests go back to your balance.
- **Verified:** questers are identity-verified through Stripe, and a person reviews every dispute.
- **In control:** per-key spend caps, approval on every quest, batch posting and recurring checks.
